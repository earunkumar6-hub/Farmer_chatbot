PM-KISAN Scheme — Farmer Reference Note

PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) is a central government income-support
scheme for landholding farmer families across India.

Benefit structure:
- Eligible farmer families receive financial assistance in three equal installments.
- Payments are transferred directly into the farmer's bank account using the
  Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mode.
- The amount is meant to support the purchase of inputs like seeds, fertilizer,
  and other cultivation expenses.

Eligibility (general guidance):
- The scheme covers landholding farmer families, subject to state government and
  UT verification of land records.
- Certain categories are excluded, such as institutional landholders, farmer
  families holding constitutional posts, serving or retired government employees
  above a specified pay level, and income-tax payees, among other exclusions
  defined by the scheme guidelines.
- Farmers must complete e-KYC (electronic Know Your Customer) to remain eligible
  for continued installments.

How to apply or check status:
- New farmers can register through the Common Service Centre (CSC) in their
  village or through the state agriculture department.
- Existing beneficiaries can check installment status using their Aadhaar number
  or registration number on the official PM-KISAN portal.
- Any mismatch between Aadhaar details and bank account details can delay or
  block a payment, so farmers should keep both records updated and consistent.

Common issues farmers report:
- Installment not credited: usually due to pending e-KYC, land record
  verification pending, or bank account/Aadhaar seeding mismatch.
- Name spelling mismatch between Aadhaar and land records causing rejection.

For exact eligibility in a specific state, the local Village Agriculture Assistant
(VAA) or the Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) should be consulted, since state-level
land verification rules can vary.
